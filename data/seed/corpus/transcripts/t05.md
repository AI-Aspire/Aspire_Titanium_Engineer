# Transcript t05

**user:** "I’m getting a timeout when I try to ping the staging DB over the VPN even though my connection shows as active. What setting am I missing?"

**assistant:** The issue is likely with your **split tunnel** setting. When split tunneling is enabled, traffic to local or specific internal networks (like staging) may not be routed through the VPN.

To fix this:
1. Open your VPN client settings.
2. Navigate to **Connection Settings** > **Advanced**.
3. Ensure that **"Split Tunnel"** is turned **OFF**, or specifically add the staging database IP range to the "Include in tunnel" list if the option allows.

If you've checked this setting and are still experiencing timeouts, let me know, and I can open a ticket for further investigation.
