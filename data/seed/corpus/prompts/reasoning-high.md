# Prompt pattern: reasoning: high

## Example 1

**Input**

I need my car cleaned. The car wash is fifty metres away. Should I walk or drive?

**Output**

You should **drive the car**. 

Since the goal is to get the car cleaned, it actually needs to be at the car wash. Walking would leave your vehicle behind! At 50 meters, you'll barely use any fuel or time, but just drive slowly and carefully since you're so close to other cars, pedestrians, or infrastructure. 

(Unless it's a mobile car wash that comes to you, in which case staying put makes sense—but based on your wording, driving is the clear choice.)
