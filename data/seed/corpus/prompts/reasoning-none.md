# Prompt pattern: reasoning: none

## Example 1

**Input**

I need my car cleaned. The car wash is fifty metres away. Should I walk or drive?

**Output**

You'll actually need to **drive** it there. Since a car wash needs the vehicle itself to clean it, walking wouldn't get your car washed unless they offer pickup/drop-off or mobile service. 

At just 50 meters, you won't even need to fully start the engine in many cases (just roll it in neutral or drive slowly), and it'll take less than a minute. So hop in, roll it over, and you'll be back on your way quickly! 🚗💦
