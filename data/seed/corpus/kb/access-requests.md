# Access requests

## Entitlements

Access is granted per entitlement, not per system. The common ones:

| Entitlement | Grants | Approver | Typical wait |
|---|---|---|---|
| `warehouse-read` | read access to the analytics warehouse | the data platform lead | one working day |
| `warehouse-write` | write access to the warehouse | the data platform lead and your manager | two working days |
| `staging-deploy` | deploying to staging | your team lead | same day |
| `prod-deploy` | deploying to production | the release manager | after a change review |
| `admin-laptop` | local administrator on your laptop | IT security | one working day, justification required |

## How to request

Open the access portal, search the entitlement name, and give a one-line
reason. The approver is notified. You are emailed when it is granted.

## Why a request sits

Most delays are a missing reason or a manager on leave. Check the portal
before filing a ticket; it shows who the request is waiting on.
