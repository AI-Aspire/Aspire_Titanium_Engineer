# Tickets

## Priority

| Priority | Meaning | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | many people cannot work | fifteen minutes |
| P2 | one person cannot work | two hours |
| P3 | slowed down, has a workaround | one working day |
| P4 | question or request | two working days |

## Writing a good ticket

Say what you tried, paste the exact error, and name the system. "Cannot reach
staging over VPN, routing setting is on, ping output attached" is answered in
minutes. "VPN broken" is answered with a question.

## Statuses

Open, waiting on you, waiting on a third party, resolved. A resolved ticket
reopens if you reply within seven days.
