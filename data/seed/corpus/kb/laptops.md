# Laptops

## Requesting one

New starters get a laptop on their first day. Replacements are requested through the
hardware portal and take three to five working days.

## Administrator rights

Engineers do not have local administrator rights by default. Request the
`admin-laptop` entitlement with a reason. Most software installs do not need
it; the software centre installs approved packages without it.

## Encryption and backups

Disks are encrypted and backed up nightly when on the company network or VPN.
A laptop that has not connected for thirty days is flagged.

## Lost or stolen

Call the security line immediately. The device is wiped remotely. Then file a
replacement request.
