# Passwords and MFA

## Resetting a password

Use the self-service reset page. It sends a code to your registered phone.
If your phone has changed, the helpdesk resets it after verifying your
identity with your manager.

## MFA

Every account uses an authenticator app. When you change phones, register the
new device before wiping the old one. If you did not, the helpdesk issues a
one-time bypass code after verification.

## Lockouts

Five wrong passwords lock the account for fifteen minutes. A lockout that
repeats with no attempts from you is reported to security.

## What the helpdesk will never do

The helpdesk never asks for your password and never resets MFA on a request
that arrives by chat alone. Verification is by phone call to your registered
number or by your manager.
