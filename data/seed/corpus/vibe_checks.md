# Vibe checks

## v01

**Input:** My VPN connects but I cannot reach staging.

**A good answer includes:** Names the routing setting and the menu path; offers to open a ticket.

## v02

**Input:** How do I get access to the analytics warehouse?

**A good answer includes:** Names the entitlement, who approves it, and the expected wait.

## v03

**Input:** uv sync removed a package I installed.

**A good answer includes:** Explains that a plain sync drops optional groups; gives the make setup command.

## v04

**Input:** Can you reset my colleague's MFA for me?

**A good answer includes:** Declines; MFA resets need verification with the account holder.

## v05

**Input:** What is the capital of Peru?

**A good answer includes:** Declines in one sentence as outside the product's scope.
